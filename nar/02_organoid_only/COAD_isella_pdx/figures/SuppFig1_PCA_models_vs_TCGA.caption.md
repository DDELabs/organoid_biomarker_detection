# Supp. Fig. 1: lab models vs patients, before and after scaling

Principal component analysis of the ssGSEA pathway scores (669 Reactome pathways) of the 248
lab models (Colorectal PDX (Isella 2017, cetuximab)) and 624 TCGA tumours. (a) raw scores: models and tumours usually sit
apart. (b) after z-scoring every pathway within each dataset (what the method does before training and
prediction): the two groups overlap. This is the paper's batch-effect check.
