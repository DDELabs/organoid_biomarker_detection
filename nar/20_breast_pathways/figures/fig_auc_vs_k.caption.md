**AUC against number of pathways kept.** Each point is the average AUC over 13 breast cohorts, each time predicting a cohort the model never saw. AUC is a score from 0.5 (coin flip) to 1 (perfect). (a) Pathways are chosen and the model refit using the 12 training cohorts only. Blue: kept in order of model weight. Green: kept in order of how well each pathway works alone. Grey: random pathways (20 draws each). Shaded bands are 95% confidence ranges. Dashed line: all 673 pathways (0.752); dotted line: 0.01 below it. (b) Pathways added one at a time, each step picking the one that helps most in inner cross-validation inside the training cohorts. Curve flattens near 0.72 after about 5 pathways.
