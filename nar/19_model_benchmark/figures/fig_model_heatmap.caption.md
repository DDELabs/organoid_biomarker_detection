**Figure (a). AUC of every model on every held-out breast cohort.** Each row is a model, each column a breast cohort the model never saw during training or tuning. Colour and number show the AUC (0.5 = coin flip, 1.0 = perfect). Rows are sorted by the mean over the 13 cohorts (last column). "Proximity shortlist" = only the 183 pathways close to the drug targets in the paper's network table. "Random pathway sets" = average of 200 random sets of 183 pathways. ER+HER2 only has 9 cohorts (receptor data missing in the others).
