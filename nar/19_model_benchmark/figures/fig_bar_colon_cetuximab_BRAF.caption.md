# Colon cetuximab vs BRAF V600E: every model against the original method

Colon cetuximab: BRAF V600E mutants vs wild type. Each bar is one model. The bar shows the main score on held-out data (AUC (mutant vs wild type); 0.5 = chance), the black line is its 95% confidence interval
(bootstrap over patients). The red bar is the original method (paper rank-1 pathway); the orange bars are other published-style methods of the same study;
blue bars are the models added here (trained with settings chosen only inside the training data); grey bars are simple baselines and the median of 200 random pathway sets (with its 2.5-97.5% range as the line).
The text at the right gives the paired difference to the original method and its bootstrap p-value (same patients for both).
Original method: 0.640. Highest bar: L1 shortlist at 0.704. Source: tables/positives_all_models.tsv.
