**Paper Fig. 3 (b-f) replica for BLCA organoids (paper data) | cisplatin.** Each panel trains one model on the 9 lab models and applies it to the
79 TCGA patients given cisplatin (20 deaths). Patients are split at the median predicted resistance;
blue = predicted responders, red = predicted non-responders; log-rank p and hazard ratio (non-responders vs responders, 95% CI).
(a) the paper's network method: the network-proximal pathway with the largest ridge weight (transport of glucose and other sugars bile salts and organic acids me…).
(b) ridge on every gene. (c) ridge on every Reactome pathway. (d) ridge on the drug targets and their direct STRING neighbours.
(e) ridge on genes picked by the Bolis et al. correlation filter (Spearman p < 0.05 with response in all 10 random halves of
the lab models; 10 genes). (f) MOLI-style deep network (encoder + classifier, cross-entropy + triplet loss),
lab models split at the median response; best of 32 hyper-parameter settings by 3-fold CV AUC (0.83; Supp Table 4).
