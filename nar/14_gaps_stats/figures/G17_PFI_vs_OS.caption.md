**Figure G17. The 57-analysis survey with progression-free interval (PFI) instead of overall survival (OS).**
PFI = time until the cancer grows, comes back, or the patient dies (TCGA Clinical Data Resource). Same patients, same
frozen organoid scores and median splits as the OS survey; only the outcome changes.
(a) Events per analysis: PFI gives more events than OS (median 29 vs 17).
(b) Treated-patient log-rank p with OS (x) and PFI (y); dotted lines = p 0.05; red/labelled = p < 0.05 with either
endpoint. OS: 7 of 57 below 0.05; PFI: 8 of 57
(about 2.9 expected by chance). (c) Hazard ratio per SD of the continuous score with OS and PFI.
