**Supplementary figure for BLCA_GEMCITABINE (bladder, gemcitabine).**
Lab models: bladder_lee2018:GEMCITABINE n=11 dropped_axes=6. Design: 5-fold cross-fitting by patient within the single cohort (5 repeats).
(a) Held-out concordance per cohort using the 36 network-proximal pathways (C-index for survival, AUROC for response; 0.5 = chance) for organoid-only (orange), patient-only (light blue) and joint (green) models. (b) The same using all 673 pathways. (c) Treatment x score interaction (ratio < 1: the score matters more in drug-treated patients than in the comparison group), network shortlist.
(d, e) Hazard ratio per SD of the score in held-out treated patients with 95% CI, shortlist and all pathways. (f) Interaction, all pathways.
Remaining rows: Kaplan-Meier curves in each held-out survival cohort, treated patients split at the cohort median score, one column per model (shortlist features). Log-rank p in each title.
