**Supplementary figure for PAAD_GEMCITABINE (pancreas, gemcitabine).**
Lab models: pancreas_tiriac2018:GEMCITABINE n=36 dropped_axes=3;pancreas_shi2022:GEMCITABINE n=39 dropped_axes=4. Design: 5-fold cross-fitting by patient within the single cohort (5 repeats).
(a) Held-out concordance per cohort using the 36 network-proximal pathways (C-index for survival, AUROC for response; 0.5 = chance) for organoid-only (orange), patient-only (light blue) and joint (green) models. (b) The same using all 673 pathways. (c) Treatment x risk-score interaction, network shortlist. Drug-specific direction: interaction HR or OR above 1.
(d, e) Hazard ratio per SD of the risk score in held-out treated patients with 95% CI, shortlist and all pathways (above 1 = expected). (f) Interaction, all pathways.
Remaining rows: Kaplan-Meier curves in each held-out survival cohort, treated patients split at the cohort median score, one column per model (shortlist features). Log-rank p in each title.
