**Supplementary figure for LIHC_SORAFENIB (liver, sorafenib).**
Lab models: liver_ji2023:SORAFENIB n=61 dropped_axes=4;liver_broutier2017:SORAFENIB n=5 dropped_axes=4. Design: 5-fold cross-fitting by patient within the single cohort (5 repeats).
(a) Held-out concordance per cohort using the 22 network-proximal pathways (C-index for survival, AUROC for response; 0.5 = chance) for organoid-only (orange), patient-only (light blue) and joint (green) models. (b) The same using all 673 pathways. (c) Treatment x score interaction, network shortlist. Drug-specific direction: interaction HR below 1 (survival) or interaction OR above 1 (response).
(d, e) Hazard ratio per SD of the score in held-out treated patients with 95% CI, shortlist and all pathways. (f) Interaction, all pathways.
Remaining rows: Kaplan-Meier curves in each held-out survival cohort, treated patients split at the cohort median score, one column per model (shortlist features). Log-rank p in each title.
