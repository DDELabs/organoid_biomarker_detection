**Alignment of lab models with patient tumours (lung).** Each dot is one sample. Grey dots are patient tumours from all cohorts used for lung; coloured dots are lab models (organoids, PDX or cell lines). Top row (a-c): principal component analysis (PCA, the two directions of largest spread). Bottom row (d-f): UMAP (a map that keeps close neighbours close). Column 1: raw pathway scores - lab models sit apart from tumours and cohorts measured on different platforms also sit apart. Column 2: every pathway standardised inside each cohort and inside each lab set (mean 0, SD 1). Column 3: standardised and then aligned - model-only directions are removed (directions with much more spread in lab models than in tumours, for example missing immune and stromal cells) and the lab-model covariance is matched to the tumour covariance (CORAL). The title number 'model-vs-tumour AUROC' is how well a classifier can tell lab models from tumours (1 = perfectly, 0.5 = cannot); lower means better mixing.
