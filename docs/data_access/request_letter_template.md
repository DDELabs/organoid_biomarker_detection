On behalf of [Institution], I request access to the datasets listed above, which were generated by [first author et al., journal, year], for the non-commercial research project described below.

**Project title.** Network-based machine learning on patient-derived organoid transcriptomes to discover pathway biomarkers of patient drug response.

**Research use statement.** Patient-derived organoids (PDOs) often reproduce the drug response of the patient they come from. The molecular programmes behind this are poorly understood. Our group (DDELabs, [Institution]) builds network-based machine-learning models. These models combine organoid gene expression (and, where available, mutation and copy-number data) with organoid drug-response measurements, then project the signal onto protein-interaction and pathway networks. The goal is to derive compact pathway-level biomarkers. We then test whether these biomarkers, learned in organoids, predict clinical response (for example RECIST response, PFS or OS) in the matched patients of your cohort. The requested data will be used only to:
1. compute expression and variant features of organoids (and tumours where available);
2. link them to the published organoid drug-response and patient-outcome annotations;
3. train and validate predictive models and compare them with existing biomarkers.
